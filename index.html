Document buried lines and equipment from your phone.

Trace a line by walking it, drop pins on valves, wells and tanks, and share one live map with your crew and the office. Import the company's KMZ, mark what you find, and hand back a clean file. Works with no cell service.

Open the web app Get GroundDraft
A documentation tool. GroundDraft records what you and your crew mark. It does not locate or verify buried facilities, and locations are approximate. Always call 811 before digging.

Built for field crews

Walk it or mark the bends

Trace a line with GPS as you walk, or stand at each bend and tap. Wobble is straightened automatically, and a pro GPS receiver can be used for tighter accuracy.

Pins for the equipment

Block valves, drips, risers, meters, wells, tanks, pumping units, compressors, panels and more, with their own fields like size, pressure rating and install date.

Your KMZ, your way

Open KMZ, KML, GeoJSON, GPX, CSV and shapefiles of any size. Folders, colors, icons and names come through like Google Earth, and every change exports back cleanly.

One map for the whole crew

Crew members join with a company code, admins approve them, and everyone's work syncs to one map. Clients can get view-only access to the jobs you choose.

No service? No problem

Jobs, lines and pins live on the phone and sync when you're back in coverage. Save the map for a site before heading out.

Office ready

Open the same jobs on any computer, clean up imported files, show and hide folders, review history, and export for Google Earth, ArcGIS, QGIS, Excel or handheld GPS.
