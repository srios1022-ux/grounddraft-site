How it works

From the field to the office in three steps

No GIS training needed. If your crew can use a phone, they can map a line.

Start a job or bring your KMZ

Create a job for a lease, gathering system or project, or open the company's KMZ, KML, GeoJSON, GPX, CSV or shapefile. Folders, colors and icons come through like Google Earth.

Mark it in the field

Walk the line with GPS or tap at each bend. Drop pins on block valves, drips, risers, meters and wells, and fill in size, material, MAOP, depth and install date while you're standing on it.

Share it and hand it back

Everything syncs to one company map when you're back in coverage. Export a clean KMZ, GeoJSON, CSV or Excel file for the office, GIS or your client.

FAQ

Questions crews and managers ask

How accurate are the locations?

Phone GPS is usually within about 10 to 16 feet in open sky, and less accurate near buildings, trees or equipment. A professional RTK receiver can get within inches when it's set up correctly. Every point keeps its accuracy reading, so you always know how good a mark is. GroundDraft is a documentation tool: it does not locate or verify buried facilities, and you should always call 811 before digging.

Does it work without cell service?

Yes. Jobs, lines and pins are saved on the device and sync when you're back in coverage. Save the map for an area before you head out and the background imagery is there too.

Can I bring in the KMZ files we already have?

Yes. GroundDraft opens KMZ, KML, GeoJSON, GPX, CSV and shapefiles, keeps their folders, colors, icons and names, and exports back to the same formats. If a file gives you trouble, email it to us and we'll get it working.

Who owns the data?

You and your company do. Paying customers can export every job at any time. We don't sell your data, and if a paid plan ends nothing is deleted: jobs stay viewable and exportable. See the Privacy Policy.

What devices does it run on?

iPhone (App Store version coming soon), plus any computer, tablet or Android phone through app.grounddraft.app, which you can install for its own icon and window.

How does the free trial work? Can I cancel?

Sign up and you get 7 days free. During our launch, no card is needed to start. When paid plans open, you pick a plan and pay by card or bank account (or your Apple ID on iPhone); nothing is charged until your trial ends. During the trial you can import, map, view and share with your crew; exporting unlocks with your first payment. After that you're charged automatically each month, and a receipt with the invoice is emailed after every payment. Plans are month to month, so you can cancel at any time. Clients you share jobs with never need a plan.
