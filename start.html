Getting started

Set up your company, add your crew, and start mapping. Most crews are up and running in about ten minutes.

Who does what: one person, usually a supervisor or office manager, sets up the company and becomes its admin. The admin picks the plan and handles billing. Crew members never pay, never see billing, and just sign in and map.

1. Set up the company (admin)

  1. Open app.grounddraft.app on a computer, or GroundDraft on your iPhone.
  2. Choose Set up my company, create your account, and enter your company's name.
  3. Plan & billing opens. Pick the plan for your number of people and add a card or bank account (ACH) to start the free 7-day trial. Nothing is charged until day 8, and you can cancel before then.
  4. Optional: add a memo (cost center, field office, well or lease name). It prints on every invoice and receipt.

After the trial, the plan is charged automatically each month, and a receipt with the invoice is emailed after every payment for your records.

2. Add your crew

There are two ways. Use whichever is easier.

A. Company code (easiest)

  1. Admin: open Jobs → Team. Your 6-character company code is at the top. Tap Share to text it to your crew.
  2. Each crew member opens GroundDraft, chooses Join my company, creates their account, and enters the code.
  3. Admin: they appear under Asking to join in Team. Pick their role and tap Approve.

B. Invite by email

  1. Admin: in Team, under Invite by email, enter their email, pick a role, and tap Add invite.
  2. Tell them to sign up in GroundDraft with that same email. They're in right away, with no approval step.

Lost the code, or someone left? Tap New code in Team; the old code stops working and everyone already in the company stays in. To remove someone, tap the ✕ next to their name.

Growing? An admin can move to a bigger plan any time in Plan & billing → Manage billing; the price difference is prorated. If you have more people than your plan allows, adding and editing pauses until you remove people or move up a plan. Nothing is deleted.

5. Bring in your maps and start marking

  1. Import your company's KMZ, KML, GeoJSON, GPX, CSV or shapefile from Jobs → Import map file. It's shared with everyone in the company automatically.
  2. In the field, crews trace lines and drop pins on valves, risers, meters and wells, and fill in size, pressure and depth. Everything saves on the device and reaches the rest of the crew within seconds when there's signal.
  3. To show a client a job: in Team, invite the client's email with the role Client. Then open the job, go to Jobs → Share with clients, and tick their name. They see only the jobs you tick, live, and can't change anything.
  4. Export a clean KMZ, CSV or GeoJSON for the office or GIS. Exporting is available once your plan's first payment has gone through.

Billing questions

  • How do we pay? By card or bank account (ACH), charged automatically each month. After each payment a receipt with the invoice (and your memo) is emailed to the admin who set up billing.
  • Change the card or bank account, download invoices or cancel: admins open Plan & billing → Manage billing.
  • Need a PO number, W-9 or a different billing contact? Email support@grounddraft.app.

Stuck on anything? Email support@grounddraft.app and we'll walk you through it.
